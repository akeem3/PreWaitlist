# Internal Notes — Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Status:** The approved policy is published at `/refund-policy`. That page is
the public source of truth; this file records supporting research and
verification items only.

PreWaitlist Pro is offered for **$15 per month** on the public pricing page.
Purchases are processed through Paddle. Paddle's Buyer Terms describe Paddle as
the authorized reseller for purchases made through Paddle Checkout, and its
Buyer Refund Policy governs transaction refund requests.

## Subscriptions and cancellations

Subscriptions renew automatically for successive billing periods until
canceled. Customers can manage or cancel a subscription through the Paddle
customer portal linked from their transaction confirmation email or the
PreWaitlist billing area. Under Paddle's Buyer Terms, cancellation takes effect
at the end of the current billing period and prevents later renewals. Access to
paid features is expected to continue through that period, consistent with the
current PreWaitlist cancellation flow.

## Refunds

Refund requests for Paddle transactions should be submitted through Paddle's
buyer support site at [Paddle.net](https://paddle.net/) or through the support
link in the customer's receipt or billing area. Refund eligibility is governed
by Paddle's [Buyer Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy),
the [Paddle Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms), and any
non-waivable rights under applicable law. Paddle's published policy states
that transactions are generally non-refundable unless required by law, while
also preserving statutory rights and allowing discretionary refunds.
